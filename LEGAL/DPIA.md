# Data Protection Impact Assessment: Joke Delivery

**Status:** APPROVED WITH CONDITIONS
**Date:** 2018-04-16

## 1. Nature of processing

The system delivers a joke to a natural person. The joke itself contains no
personal data. However, the *fact that a joke was delivered to a specific
person at a specific time*, together with any laughter recorded by
`laugh-service`, constitutes personal data within the meaning of Art. 4(1).

## 2. Lawful basis

Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)). The legitimate interest is amusement.

## 3. Identified risks

| Risk | Severity | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Subject does not find the joke funny | Low | None available |
| Laughter is recorded without consent | High | Consent banner (see condition 1) |
| Joke is retained indefinitely | Medium | Retention policy, 30 days |
| Subject requests erasure of the joke | Medium | See condition 2 |

## 4. Conditions

1. A consent banner MUST be displayed before the joke is told.
2. An erasure mechanism MUST be implemented.

## 5. Residual risk

Accepted.
